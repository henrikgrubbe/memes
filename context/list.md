# Saga: List

- The saga follows a group of adventurers known as the Wanderers, who travel across various realms in search of legendary artifacts.
- The main characters include:
  - **Aria**, a skilled archer with a mysterious past.
  - **Bram**, a bumbling mage with a penchant for exploding spells.
  - **Cora**, a fierce warrior with loyalty to her friends and a secret lineage as a dragon.
  - **Dax**, a charming rogue whose quick wit often gets the group into trouble.
- They frequently visit the mystical town of Eldermere, known for its ancient library and magical shops.
- The group's primary goal is to locate the seven lost shards of the Celestial Stone, rumored to grant the power to reshape reality.
- The adventures often lead them to the enchanted forest of Liora, where they encounter mischievous fairies and treacherous beasts.
- Running jokes include:
  - Bram's constant failure with spells, often leading to comedic explosions.
  - Dax's flamboyant stories about his past exploits, which grow increasingly absurd.
  - Aria's fierce rivalry with a fellow archer named Elenora, who always seems to one-up her.
- Key story beats include:
  - The discovery of the first shard in an ancient temple guarded by a Sphinx.
  - Their first encounter with a rival group of treasure hunters known as the Shadow Seekers.
  - A betrayal within their ranks that tests their bonds of friendship.
- The Wanderers are united by their quest for the Celestial Stone but also by their growing camaraderie and the challenges they face together.
