# Canon: henriksg 

- Henrik is a character who believes he cannot live without his G. 
- The significance of 'G' remains a mystery and serves as a central theme throughout the saga. 
- The phrase "henrik kan ikke leve uden sit G" becomes a recurring line, emphasizing Henrik's dependency. 

# Recurring Characters
- Henrik: The protagonist, struggling with his attachment to 'G'.
- Supporting Characters: Various friends and acquaintances who often comment on Henrik's obsession, providing comic relief and insight into his character.

# Key Locations
- Henrik's Apartment: A cluttered space filled with reminders of 'G'.
- Local Café: A hangout spot where Henrik and friends discuss life, love, and 'G'.
  
# Running Jokes
- Henrik's friends often tease him about his need for 'G', but also show genuine concern for his well-being. 
- A common joke involves Henrik trying various ridiculous methods to cope without 'G', each leading to comedic disasters. 

# Key Story Beats
- Henrik's realization of his dependency on 'G' prompts him to embark on a journey of self-discovery.
- Encounters with various characters who embody what 'G' represents lead to moments of reflection for Henrik.
- Climactic moments where Henrik must confront the idea of living without 'G' and what that truly means for him. 

- The saga unfolds as Henrik navigates the ups and downs of his fixation while exploring the deeper meaning of his attachment to 'G'.
