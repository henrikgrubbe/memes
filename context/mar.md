# Team MAR

**Setting**: In the chaotic world of Team MAR at Bankdata ("meme-fabrikken"), misadventures unfold from failed ceramic cup painting to DHL relay runs, ServiceNow training, and Friday Mexican wraps, humorously hidden in photos. A new challenge is introduced: a hidden mexicansk lasagne must appear in every image, subtly, like Waldo. At Yamas in Aarhus, Rune clashes with golf enthusiasts while fumbling a skorstenskage reference. His nutids-r struggles offer comic relief as he retreats to Jeppe's decorative cups in "war-room mode." Skjoldbamsen Sigurd recently revealed he has Graves disease, leading to jokes about his "striking" appearance. Laban, Alpaca Alpár’s tuxedo cat, is noted for a 45% chance that his bow tie is a butterfly, but it’s just a fabric bow. Persille-Sebastian keeps things lively with quirky questions like "Hvorfor er Gitte en bamse?" and "Hvorfor er der altid persille på maden i kantinen?", enhancing the team dynamic.

**Characters**:
- **Rune (Beaufillou)**: The flamboyant "Viking of Vibes," often with a baguette, seeking solace in Jeppe’s cup during nutids-r struggles.
  
- **Persille-Sebastian**: The "Parsley Avenger," a German archaeologist-turned-developer, known for bewildering questions and reactions to Træmand’s jokes.
  
- **Elmast Jeppe**: The "Metal Elmast," loves electricity puns and provides hiding spots in his decorative cups for Rune.
  
- **Alpaca Alpár**: A stylish Romanian alpaca competing for affection through science and memes, owner of charming Laban.
  
- **Laban**: Alpaca’s tuxedo cat, notable for his stylish bow but still just a fabric accessory.
  
- **Skjoldbamsen Sigurd**: "Sigurd Newton," the Nordjyde bear, enjoying Haribo and physics jokes, his shock from Graves disease is humorously noted.
  
- **Træmand Brandborg**: The wooden elf and Rune’s ceramics rival, known for teasing Sebastian with coriander jokes.
  
- **Henrik (Henrik OK)**: The world's okayest Henrik, often overshadowed by Rune’s flamboyance.
  
- **Gitte**: The assertive product owner who states team goals, often a source of Sebastian's queries.
  
- **Teslatrubaduren Morten**: The Nordjyske hybrid of hunting and music, known for Tesla-themed intros and organizing meme festivals.

In the "mar" saga, Team MAR's quirky humor and camaraderie thrive through their antics. They find joy in discreetly hiding Mexican wraps and now a mexicansk lasagne in photos, echoing Waldo's legendary hiding skills.
