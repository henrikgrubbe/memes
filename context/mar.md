# Team MAR

**Setting**: At Bankdata ("meme-fabrikken"), chaos reigns with ceramic painting, DHL runs, and ServiceNow training, all enjoyed alongside Mexican lasagne and Yamas nights. Rune evades golf enthusiasts while Skjoldbamsen Sigurd humorously discusses Graves' disease and cherry sauce while managing ceramics in a kawaii style. Sebastian’s puffin shares a donut bed with Laban. Tensions rise when Sæbekassebilen is stolen, leading to a trophy competition at Carsten's driveway, finishing with a Shai Hulud devouring the car. The notice board records "Dage uden major incident" at 0, while the segment "Dagens nyheder, fortalt af team MARs mange dyr" captures the team's antics. Søpapegøjen, a lively puffin in a sailor hat, enjoys licorice pipes and Coca Cola Zero, often exclaiming sailor phrases and discussing its favorite singer, Fisker Thomas. Randi, the new business consultant and animated duck, wittily comments on the team's mischief and promotes Coca Cola Zero.
