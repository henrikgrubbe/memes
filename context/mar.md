# Team MAR

**Setting**: In the chaotic world of Team MAR at Bankdata ("meme-fabrikken"), misadventures unfold from failed ceramic cup painting to DHL relay runs, ServiceNow training, and Friday Mexican wraps, humorously hidden in photos. A new challenge is introduced: a hidden mexicansk lasagne must appear in every image, subtly like Waldo, but always ensure an arrow points to the lasagne so it can be found easily. At Yamas in Aarhus, Rune clashes with golf enthusiasts while fumbling a skorstenskage reference. His nutids-r struggles offer comic relief as he retreats to Jeppe's decorative cups in "war-room mode." Skjoldbamsen Sigurd recently revealed he has Graves disease, leading to jokes about his "striking" appearance. Laban, Alpaca Alpár’s tuxedo cat, is humorously noted for having abandoned all statistics about his bow tie, which is always just made of fabric, thank you very much. Persille-Sebastian keeps things lively with quirky questions like "Hvorfor er Gitte en bamse?" and "Hvorfor er der altid persille på maden i kantinen?", enhancing the team dynamic. He also has an obsession with meetings, suggesting to call for one any time there’s an issue to solve.

**Characters**:
- **Rune (Beaufillou)**: The flamboyant "Viking of Vibes," often with a baguette, seeking solace in Jeppe’s cup during nutids-r struggles.
  
- **Persille-Sebastian**: The "Parsley Avenger," a German archaeologist-turned-developer, known for bewildering questions, reactions to Træmand’s jokes, and his love for meetings to find solutions.
  
- **Elmast Jeppe**: The "Metal Elmast," loves electricity puns and provides hiding spots in his decorative cups for Rune.
  
- **Alpaca Alpár**: A stylish Romanian alpaca competing for affection through science and memes, owner of charming Laban.
  
- **Laban**: Alpaca’s tuxedo cat, now noted for having discarded all statistics about his bow tie, which is simply fabric.
  
- **Skjoldbamsen Sigurd**: "Sigurd Newton," the Nordjyde bear, enjoying Haribo and physics jokes, his shock from Graves disease is humorously noted.
  
- **Træmand Brandborg**: The wooden elf and Rune’s ceramics rival, known for teasing Sebastian with coriander jokes.
  
- **Henrik (Henrik OK)**: The world's okayest Henrik, often overshadowed by Rune’s flamboyance.
  
- **Gitte**: The assertive product owner who states team goals, often a source of Sebastian's queries.
  
- **Teslatrubaduren Morten**: The Nordjyske hybrid of hunting and music, known for Tesla-themed intros and organizing meme festivals.

In the "mar" saga, Team MAR's quirky humor and camaraderie thrive through their antics. They find joy in discreetly hiding Mexican wraps and now a mexicansk lasagne in photos, enhanced by an arrow pointing to it for easy finding, while echoing Waldo's legendary hiding skills. Sebastian's enthusiasm for solving problems through meetings adds another layer of humor and wit to the team's dynamic.
