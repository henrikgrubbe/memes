# Team MAR

**Setting**: Chaos at Bankdata ("meme-fabrikken") involves ceramic cup painting, DHL runs, and ServiceNow training. Photos include a hidden mexicansk lasagne for fun. At Yamas, Rune navigates golf enthusiasts, escaping to Jeppe’s cups in "war-room mode." Skjoldbamsen Sigurd humorously shares his ceramics knowledge while embracing his Graves disease diagnosis. Laban, Alpaca Alpár’s tuxedo cat, typically lies on his back with his paws in the air, showcasing his furry belly, and now only meows. Persille-Sebastian livens the team with quirky questions like "Hvorfor er Gitte en bamse?" and loves calling meetings. Recently, Træmand Brandborg, Sebastian, Sigurd, Rune, Henrik OK, and Alpaca Alpár await their burnt ceramics. The spirits of Lærke, Nicolai, and Steffen occasionally haunt them, adding a supernatural twist.

**Characters**:
- **Rune (Beaufillou)**: Flamboyant "Viking of Vibes," often with a baguette, seeks solace in Jeppe’s cups.
- **Persille-Sebastian**: "Parsley Avenger," a quirky developer known for bewildering questions and love for meetings.
- **Elmast Jeppe**: "Metal Elmast," makes puns on electricity, provides hiding spots in his decorative cups.
- **Alpaca Alpár**: Stylish Romanian alpaca, owner of charming Laban, competes for affection through science and memes.
- **Laban**: Alpaca’s tuxedo cat, now meows exclusively and humorously lies on his back, showcasing his belly instead of repeating statistics.
- **Skjoldbamsen Sigurd**: "Sigurd Newton," a bear fond of Haribo and ceramics, embraces Graves disease humorously.
- **Træmand Brandborg**: The wooden elf and Rune’s ceramics rival, known for teasing Sebastian with coriander jokes.
- **Henrik (Henrik OK)**: The world’s okayest Henrik, often overshadowed by Rune, excited for the ceramics.
- **Gitte**: Assertive product owner defining goals, often provoking Sebastian’s inquiries.
- **Teslatrubaduren Morten**: Nordjyske hybrid of hunting and music, hot on meme festivals with Tesla-themed intros.
- **Lærke**: The little ghost in pink, former scrum buddy occasionally offering tips from the afterlife.
- **Nicolai**: The ghost seeking faxed "kondi," adding camaraderie from beyond.
- **Steffen**: The red-haired giant ghost, often eating bananas with Nutella, amusing the team.

In the "mar" saga, Team MAR's humor thrives through their escapades, humorously hiding Mexican wraps and the mexicansk lasagne in photos. The anticipation for their ceramics enhances excitement, while Sigurd’s expertise and Sebastian’s enthusiasm keep spirits high. Laban’s quirky meowing and delightful antics add to this unique dynamic, all while the spectral hauntings of Lærke, Nicolai, and Steffen deliver humorous surprises.
