# Team MAR

**Setting**: In the chaotic world of Team MAR at Bankdata ("meme-fabrikken"), misadventures unfold from failed ceramic cup painting to DHL relay runs, ServiceNow training, and Friday Mexican wraps, with the humorous twist of always hiding these wraps in photos to make them difficult to find. A new challenge has emerged: there must always be a hidden mexicansk lasagne somewhere in any image generated, adding to the fun of searching for the concealed food. At Yamas in Aarhus, Rune clashes with golf enthusiasts while fumbling a skorstenskage reference. His nutids-r struggles provide comic relief as he retreats to Jeppe's decorative cups in "war-room mode." Recently, Skjoldbamsen Sigurd’s revelation of Graves disease sparked jokes about his "striking" appearance, with AI noting his perennial shock. Meanwhile, Laban, Alpaca Alpár’s tuxedo cat with a playful 45% chance that his bow tie is an actual butterfly, reminds that it’s just a fabric bow for festive events. Persille-Sebastian keeps the quirks alive with humorous questions like "Hvorfor er Gitte en bamse?" and "Hvorfor er der altid persille på maden i kantinen?", adding to the team’s dynamic.

**Characters**:
- **Rune (Beaufillou)**: The flamboyant "Viking of Vibes," often found with his baguette, uses Jeppe’s cup for solace during nutids-r struggles.

- **Persille-Sebastian**: The "Parsley Avenger," a German archaeologist turned developer, known for bewildering questions and his reactions to Træmand’s corny jokes.

- **Elmast Jeppe**: The "Metal Elmast," loves electricity puns and provides a hiding spot in his decorative cups for Rune.

- **Alpaca Alpár**: A stylish Romanian alpaca who fiercely competes with Rune for affection through science and memes, owns the charming Laban.

- **Laban**: Alpaca's tuxedo cat with striking patterns and a fabric blue butterfly accessory, reminding us it’s just a stylish bow.

- **Skjoldbamsen Sigurd**: "Sigurd Newton," the Nordjyde bear who enjoys Haribo and physics jokes, humorously noted for his shocked appearance due to Graves disease.

- **Træmand Brandborg**: The wooden elf and Rune’s ceramics rival, known for teasing Sebastian with coriander jokes and his musical flair.

- **Henrik (Henrik OK)**: The world's okayest Henrik, whose mundane introductions contrast with Rune’s flamboyance.

- **Gitte**: The assertive product owner who succinctly states team goals, often a source of Sebastian's curious inquiries.

- **Teslatrubaduren Morten**: The Nordjyske hybrid of hunting and music, known for his memorable Tesla-themed intros and planning the next meme festival.

In the saga "mar," Team MAR's quirky humor and camaraderie shine through their antics. They delight in hiding Mexican wraps and now a mexicansk lasagne in photos, adding an extra layer of fun to their meme-making antics.
