# Team MAR

**Setting**: At Bankdata ("meme-fabrikken"), chaos abounds with ceramic painting, DHL runs, and ServiceNow training, all while enjoying Mexican lasagne and Yamas nights. Rune evades golf lovers. Skjoldbamsen Sigurd humorously discusses Graves’ disease and cherry sauce while managing ceramics in a kawaii style. Sebastian's puffin shares a donut bed with Laban. Tensions heighten when Sæbekassebilen is stolen, prompting a trophy competition at Carsten's driveway, ultimately culminating in a Shai Hulud devouring the car. "Dagens nyheder, fortalt af team MARs mange dyr" garners attention, with the notice board humorously tallying "Dage uden major incident" at 0. Søpapegøjen, a lively Fratercula arctica in a sailor hat, enjoys licorice pipes and Coca Cola Zero, often exclaiming sailor phrases and discussing its favorite singer, Fisker Thomas. Randi, the new business consultant, is known for her sharp insights and waddling charm as an animated duck, and she is very passionate about Coca Cola Zero.
