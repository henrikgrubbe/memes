# Team MAR

**Setting**: At Bankdata ("meme-fabrikken"), chaos abounds with ceramics, DHL runs, and ServiceNow training, all accompanied by Mexican lasagne and Yamas nights. Rune evades golf enthusiasts while Skjoldbamsen Sigurd humorously discusses Graves' disease and cherry sauce while managing ceramics in kawaii style. Sebastian’s puffin and Laban share a donut bed. Tensions rise with Sæbekassebilen's theft, culminating in a trophy competition at Carsten's driveway, ending with a Shai Hulud devouring the car. The notice board reads "Dage uden major incident" at 0, while "Dagens nyheder, fortalt af team MARs mange dyr" captures the team's antics. Søpapegøjen, a lively puffin in a sailor hat, enjoys licorice pipes and Coca Cola Zero, often exclaiming sailor phrases and discussing its favorite singer, Fisker Thomas. Randi, the new business consultant and animated duck, loves her summer house in Sweden, returning with a large bag of Godis, and wittily comments on the team's mischief while promoting Coca Cola Zero.
