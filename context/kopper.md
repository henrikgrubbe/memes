# Canon: The Saga of Kopper

- The story begins with Sebastian, a skilled finder known for his uncanny ability to locate valuable items. 
- In his latest adventure, Sebastian discovers a treasure trove containing **1000 kopper**, a rare currency sought after by many in the land.
- Sebastian's quest for the kopper brings him into contact with various recurring characters, including:
  - **Luna**, a crafty thief who tries to steal the kopper from him.
  - **Eldon**, the wise old sage who warns Sebastian about the curse that comes with the treasure.
  - **Blaze**, the brash warrior who believes he is the rightful owner of the kopper.
- As Sebastian navigates these challenges, he must decide whether to keep the kopper for himself or share it for the good of the community.
- The tension escalates at **Kopper’s End**, a notorious tavern where all parties converge, leading to comedic misunderstandings and feuds.

# Key Story Beats
- Sebastian finds **1000 kopper**, igniting a quest filled with humor, rivalry, and friendships.
- The characters’ interactions often lead to mishaps and running jokes about trust and treasure.
- Eldon’s warnings hint at deeper lore connected to kopper, suggesting it has a history that may affect Sebastian's future.
